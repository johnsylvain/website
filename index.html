John Sylvain, Product Engineer

I'm John Sylvain, a product engineer at Runway, where I build creative tools powered by frontier AI. I'm interested in the space where engineering, art, and emerging technology meet, and in turning powerful models into products that give people new ways to make things.

Outside of work, I run Artifact Lab, an independent studio for physical computing and hardware-software hybrids. Recently, I've been repurposing obsolete technology into ambient displays and exploring how computers can take on a more physical, expressive form.

Work

MeetingWith - meetingwith.app - MeetingWith bundles scheduling, 1:1 video calls (WebRTC), and AI-enriched notes into a single tool, plus a profile page that gives you a polished web presence. I'm building it so solo professionals and small teams can book, meet, and capture follow-ups without stitching together a calendar, a video app, and a note-taker.

Freelane - usefreelane.com - Freelane turns intake notes into a proposal, contract, and payment in a single shareable link. I'm building it for freelancers to replace the patchwork of PDFs, e-signature tools, and invoice chasing.

Artifact Lab - artifactlab.net - A private design studio for physical computing, hardware prototyping, and experimental systems. Projects span physical interfaces, ambient displays, and tangible interactive objects.
